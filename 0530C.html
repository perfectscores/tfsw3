09

"I think parks are equally important for adults and children.

Green spaces can improve air quality and provide opportunities for outdoor activities. They also help people feel less stressed. In busy cities, parks are often one of the few places where people can connect with nature."

 

11

参考答案

 

"Yes, definitely. If possible, I would work from home four days a week.

 

I am someone who likes a quiet environment, and I think remote work can increase productivity. Without commuting, I could spend more time on work and personal activities. I would only go to the office occasionally for meetings or team discussions."

 

09

Thank you. Can you describe some potential challenges you might face if you were to work remotely?

 

参考答案

 

"One challenge could be separating work from personal life. When people work from home, it can be difficult to know when to stop working.

 

Another challenge is technical problems. If the internet connection fails or software does not work properly, productivity can be affected quite quickly."

 

10

Interesting. Some jobs can be done remotely while others cannot. Do you think it is fair for a company to offer some employees the opportunity to work remotely while other employees cannot? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"Yes, I think it is fair. Fairness does not necessarily mean everyone receives the same arrangement.

 

If remote work helps certain employees perform their jobs more effectively, companies should allow it. At the same time, businesses should provide other forms of support for employees whose jobs require them to be on site."

 

11

Great. Some people believe that remote working is the future of work and will become more common. Do you agree or disagree with this idea? Why?

 

参考答案

 

"I agree with this idea. As technology continues to improve, more jobs can be completed remotely.

 

Remote work can reduce commuting time, lower costs for companies, and provide employees with greater flexibility. Because of these benefits, I think it will become increasingly common over the next several years."
